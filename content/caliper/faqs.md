items:
  - question: "Which accounting systems does Caliper work with?"
    answer: "Tally today. Zoho, QuickBooks and Sage are planned, along with Excel template upload. Scope, sequence and release dates depend on development priorities."
  - question: "How often does data refresh?"
    answer: "Every 30 minutes. The Caliper Tally Connector syncs your books, so there's nothing to export or upload."   # REVIEW: confirm "nothing to export or upload"
  - question: "Do I need to change my accounting system?"
    answer: "No. Your team keeps working in Tally as it does today. The Caliper Tally Connector only reads your data. It never writes entries back to your books."
  - question: "How is my data protected?"
    answer: "The connector is read-only, and access is granted user by user and role by role. Full details on encryption, hosting, retention and backups are available for your security review. Write to [support@contelligent.in](mailto:support@contelligent.in)."
  - question: "Can I see more than one company?"
    answer: "Yes. Caliper gives you one view across companies, instead of separate files and separate logins."
  - question: "Who is Caliper built for?"
    answer: "Chartered Accountants, finance teams, business owners, and investors and lenders who want visibility across their portfolio companies."
